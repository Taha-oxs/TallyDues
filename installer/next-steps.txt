TallyDues is installed - everything that can be done for you automatically
already has been (license checked and stored, files copied, notification
engine registered). Nothing else needs running. From now on TallyDues works
without internet until your license end date.
Setup details are logged to C:\ProgramData\TallyDues\install.log.

ONE step still has to happen INSIDE TallyPrime - no Windows installer can
do it, because TallyPrime only registers add-ons through its own menu.
It takes about 10 seconds, once, on this PC:

  1. Open TallyPrime.
  2. Press F1 (Help) -> TDL and Add-On -> F4 (Manage Local TDL Files).
  3. Set "Load on startup" to Yes, then Browse to the TallyDues file
     in C:\ProgramData\TallyDues, and press Ctrl+A to accept.
  4. RESTART TallyPrime completely (close and reopen). A "Welcome to
     TallyDues" notification appears.
  5. Open the company you want TallyDues to watch. At the Gateway of
     Tally screen you now have three TallyDues menu items:
       TallyDues Run Check   check what is due now (Windows notifications)
       TallyDues Dashboard   list of due cheques/PDCs and invoices, plus
                             the reminders you have snoozed
       TallyDues Settings    change the alert timeframe (7 days back and
                             ahead until you change it)

Cheque and invoice notifications have "Snooze 1 day" and "Snooze 3 days"
buttons: click one and the same reminder comes back when the time is up.

RENEWING YOUR LICENSE: TallyDues warns you before your license ends. Click
Renew on the notification and renew on the OpenXpert portal in your browser.
Once this PC is online, TallyDues finds the renewal by itself. Your key stays
the same.

NOT SEEING NOTIFICATIONS? Windows may be hiding them (Do Not Disturb /
Focus Assist, or notifications turned off for "Windows PowerShell").
Start Menu > TallyDues > "Alerts - also show pop-up boxes" makes every
alert also appear as a pop-up box.

The user guide is in Start Menu > TallyDues > User Guide.
